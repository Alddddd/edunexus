# 3. CORE RELATIONSHIPS

## users

```text
Admin
→ creates assistance programs

Member
→ submits assistance requests

Merchant
→ verifies claims
```

---

## assistance_programs

```text
assistance_programs
→ has many assistance_requests
```

---

## assistance_requests

```text
assistance_requests
→ belongs to member
→ belongs to assistance_program
→ has one or many claims
```

---

## merchants

```text
merchants
→ has many claims
→ has many settlements
```

---

## claims

```text
claims
→ belongs to assistance_request
→ belongs to merchant
→ has settlement
```

---

## settlements

```text
settlements
→ belongs to merchant
→ belongs to claim
```

---

## blockchain_logs

```text
blockchain_logs
→ linked to approvals
→ linked to claims
→ linked to settlements
```
